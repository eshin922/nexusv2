# Five-order Nexus to NetSuite sandbox certification — 2026-09-29

The orders below were entered in the production Nexus interface for a fictional `ZZ-VALIDATION` customer, then sent to **NetSuite sandbox**. Product, cost, quote, and acceptance fields were entered through browser keystrokes; no order fields were pasted or inserted directly through a database/API. Before each acceptance and order send, the certification endpoint reported HubSpot accept synchronization `SUPPRESSED`, NetSuite environment `sandbox`, sandbox account `true`, and sandbox certification writes authorized.

NetSuite readback used SuiteQL against the sandbox account and checked each Sales Order's saved transaction total and line quantities/rates. NetSuite's `transactionline` readback presents quantities and net amounts with negative signs; the magnitudes below are the ordered values.

The fifth order initially blocked because Tooling & Dies was not classified. Classifying it as **Mould / collar** resolved to `OTC-0006`, and the revised v2 quote posted with the charge as a distinct NetSuite line. This validated the classification guard. The case also exposed two Costs navigation defects addressed in this change: excluding Freight hid the unrelated Packaging editor, and the main Costs view had no visible route to edit a service's Production cost.

Remaining coverage limits: `otc_raws` is still unmapped because no suitable sandbox inventory item was identified; a separate Master Carton charge and separately billed freight/customs were not generated by these five orders. Their configured destinations should be verified with Accounting before claiming those paths are certified.
