# Slice 13 training and organizational-readiness plan

## Common deliverables

Each audience receives a role-specific slide deck, user guide, one-page quick
reference, recorded walkthrough, hands-on sandbox exercise, FAQ, onboarding
module, attendance/competency record, and named sign-off. Final material must
match the release candidate and link to authoritative operating procedures.

| Audience | Required content and exercise | Required sign-off |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | Quote/customer lifecycle, HubSpot source quality, acceptance, visible pricing, escalation | Sales leader |
| Operations | Completion handoff, Item Groups, quantities/dates, exceptions, no manual workaround concealment | Operations leader |
| Accounting | SO field lineage, rates/totals/tax/terms, intentional differences, reconciliation, rollback disposition | Accounting leader |
| Project Management | End-to-end quote → acceptance → tier → completion, mappings, retries, audit evidence | PM leader |
| IT | Environments, credentials, integrations, monitoring, incident/rollback, ownership and access review | IT/security owner |
| Executives | Business controls, readiness metrics, accepted risks, Go/No-Go and hypercare governance | Executive sponsor |

## Delivery sequence

1. Approve process and parity contracts.
2. Produce role-based materials from the release candidate.
3. Run instructor walkthroughs and sandbox exercises.
4. Assess competency using observable outcomes, not attendance alone.
5. Resolve questions into the FAQ and update onboarding material.
6. Collect all department sign-offs before production Go/No-Go.

## Minimum hands-on outcomes

- Sales/PM: create through completion without bypassing lifecycle controls.
- Operations/Accounting: locate and reconcile a correlated sandbox Sales Order,
  including Item Group and commercial rate behavior.
- IT: identify environment/account, observe a failure, preserve evidence, stop
  processing safely, and execute the rollback drill.
- Executives: interpret readiness status, blockers, exceptions, and decision
  rights.

## Ownership and open questions

Named trainers, approvers, training environment, accessibility/localization
needs, scheduling, knowledge-check thresholds, and evidence-retention location
are **Requires manual discovery**. No department may self-waive a blocking
control without the documented Go/No-Go authority.
