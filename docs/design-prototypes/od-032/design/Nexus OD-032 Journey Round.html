01 · the journey, as one motion

The operator has one thought. It crosses three surfaces.

“This carton causes plates, dies and samples.” That sentence is authored in Setup, read as cost in Costs, and priced in Commercial Recovery. The design's job across the journey is that it stays one sentence — the same charge, recognisable at each stop, never re-explained.

The two decisions stay apart at every stop. Setup asks what was caused and what it cost. Recovery asks who pays. The sheet never asks for placement, and the rail never asks for cost — fusing them is the one thing the model exists to prevent.

02 · Setup — preserved, not redesigned

The only surface the Design Authority draws in full

Phases 1 and 2 of the round trip's §03 are canonical and this round changes nothing about them: multi-select checklist, up to three suggestion chips from product type, never pre-checked; then one staging table pricing every selected row together, cost per tier, fixed one-time basis, placement not asked.

The measured constraint that matters: the assembly tree has no row-level ··· menu today, so the entry affordance is new. Everything behind it is drawn.

Preserved verbatim

Suggestion is a prompt, selection is an act. Same type twice is legal — a warning with a required distinct label, never a block. Deleting the component names its charges and their total first.

The sharpest risk, carried in copy

Template memory will be read as price memory. The sheet says the library remembers types; amounts are entered per quote, every time — and no prior amount is ever shown as a prefill, not even greyed. A greyed prefill is a prefill.

Constraint this round adds

The V1 sheet offers component types only. No quote-owned type is selectable, which is what keeps the missing Project region unreachable rather than unhandled.

04 · Commercial Recovery

The layout is right. The grain is what changes.

Measured: the recovery surface addresses charges by type in 22 references across three files, and no component references an instance. So the change is not visual — a row still reads as a charge with a placement — it is what a row is.

Why per instance is not negotiable

Print plates on one carton must be able to differ from print plates on another — two causes, and one may be absorbed as a concession. Rolling recovery up by type makes that concession unrepresentable, which is a commercial capability lost to a data-modelling convenience.

Why the group action is not a nicety

Without it a uniform quote costs one click per charge, and the operator's shortcut becomes absorbing things to make the rail quiet — a margin event chosen for interface reasons. Grain per instance, ergonomics per group.

Disambiguation, only where needed

The owner name appears beside the type only when two charges of one type are present. One Print plates row reads “Print plates”. This mirrors the customer document's collision rule, so internal and external copy agree.

05 · what this round did not decide

Left open on purpose

OpenWhy it is not settled here
Which Costs alternativeBoth are drawn and costed. Choosing between them is a judgement about how much a certified page may change during training — not an engineering call.
The standalone one-time regionBelongs to the post-V1 Costs redesign, which reconsiders the page as one system. Designing it now would be the accidental permanent architecture this round exists to avoid.
Duty and customs placementSame redesign. They are freight economics, not charges under this model, and moving them is a Freight change.
Absorption governanceToday only the result is governed. Owner attribution makes five absorptions in one quote easy where it used to be occasional. Result-governance is still the recommendation, but the exposure is larger than the policy was written against.
Project setup's true ownerExisting rows are Item-Group-owned by storage and BV-012; a newly authored one would be quote-owned. V1 accepts the split by age; the redesign should decide whether the vocabulary entry is what is wrong.
